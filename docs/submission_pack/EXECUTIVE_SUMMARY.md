## 💡 Executive Brief

**ChainStory** transforms unreadable, hexadecimal blockchain transaction logs into human-readable plain-English stories, screens wallets for counterparty risk and activity diversity, and generates **DRAFT Form 8949 / Schedule D capital gains tax estimates** to review with a CPA.

### Key Value Pillars:
1. **AI Plain-English Story Generator**: 1-sentence descriptions powered by Google Gemini (JSON Schema mode) with deterministic keyword fallback.
2. **Wallet Intelligence & Reputation**: Transparent wallet age, activity diversity, protocol count, and explainable reputation labels (*Established Veteran*, *DeFi Explorer*, *Regular Active*, *Newly Created*).
3. **Counterparty Risk & Approvals Transparency**: Screen counterparties against Tornado Cash and sanctions databases, plus a read-only Token Approvals History panel.
4. **Draft Form 8949 Tax Engine**: Multi-chain FIFO cost-basis accounting with 1-click CSV and printable PDF tax report exports.
5. **Phase 2 Pre-Scan Risk Explainer**: Pre-interaction risk scanner analyzing token contract age, verification status, and proxy upgradeability in plain English.
