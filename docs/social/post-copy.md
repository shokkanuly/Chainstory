## Threads — main post

Threads rewards a slower, first-person voice. Same facts, less staccato.
Attach `poster-plain-english.png`.

> I got tired of squinting at block explorers.
>
> You open one to answer a simple question — what did I actually do with this
> wallet? — and you get hex strings and wei. The data is all there. It's just
> not written for people.
>
> So I built ChainStory. You paste an address and it reads back the history
> in plain English: "Swapped 2.0 ETH for 3,400 USDC on Uniswap V3." Same
> transaction, translated.
>
> Once the transactions are readable, the rest follows from the same pipeline
> — a draft tax report with FIFO cost basis, a list of every token approval
> still able to move your funds, and a risk check on a contract before you
> sign it.
>
> It's read-only. You never connect a wallet. Five EVM chains.
>
> It's also a beta, and the tax output is a draft — it only reads your last
> 100 transactions, so older cost basis can be missing. I'd rather say that
> upfront than have someone file from it.
>
> It's open source. If you paste an address and it gets something wrong, that
> is exactly what I want to hear.
>
> https://retold-nu.vercel.app
